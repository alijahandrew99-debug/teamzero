## Title

**Independent Sales Representative — AI Phone Answering Software (1099, Commission, Remote US)**

## Body

Dawnpipe is an AI receptionist for small service businesses — plumbers, HVAC,
electricians, roofers, auto shops, salons. It answers the calls the owner
misses, asks what's wrong, and books the job into their calendar. Our
customers are one-to-fifteen-person businesses where the owner is also the
person who can't get to the phone.

We're looking for independent sales representatives to sell it. This is a
1099 commission role. There is no base, no draw, and no guaranteed income.

**The role**
- Find small service businesses that are missing calls, and show them what
  answering those calls is worth.
- Demo the product by having the owner call our AI and hear it for themselves.
- Close them onto a 7-day free trial on their own phone number.
- Keep the accounts you sign; they stay attributed to you.

**Commission**
- 30% of what we collect from your accounts each month, for their first 24
  months, then 10% for as long as the account stays.
- Our main plan is $499/month, so one account pays **$149.70 per month** while
  it stays active. Other plans run $99–$1,499, paying $29.70–$449.70.
- Paid monthly on revenue actually collected. Nothing accrues during a
  customer's free trial. Refunds and chargebacks reverse.
- We have no earnings history to share. See the disclosure below.

**You'd be a fit if you**
- Have sold to small business owners before — B2B, SaaS, telecom, insurance,
  advertising, or business services.
- Can prospect without being managed.
- Are comfortable on the phone with a tradesperson who has thirty seconds.

**What we provide**
- Your own referral link and code, so every sale you make is tracked to you.
- A demo line you can have any prospect call, right now.
- A one-page brief with the pitch and the three objections you'll hear.
- A free Dawnpipe account so you can use the product yourself.

**Disclosure**
This is an independent contractor (1099) commission-only opportunity, not
employment. There is no base salary, no draw, and no guaranteed income.
Dawnpipe has no paying customers yet and no sales representative has yet
earned a commission, so we have no data on what a representative typically
earns. Your earnings depend entirely on how many customers you acquire and
whether they stay. Most people in commission-only sales earn little or
nothing. Commission is 30% of collected subscription revenue for an account's
first 24 months, then 10%, net of refunds, chargebacks, discounts and tax.

**To apply:** email <careers@dawnpipe.com> with a short note about what you've
sold before. Call our AI first if you like — the number is on dawnpipe.com —
so you know what you'd be selling.
