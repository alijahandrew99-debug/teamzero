## ✅ You MAY say these, and only these, about the product

**Answering**
- It answers inbound calls 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays.
- It answers in a natural, human-sounding voice.
- It tells every caller that it is an AI.
- It only says what the business owner has told it — their prices, services, and hours.
- If it doesn't know something, it says so and takes a message rather than guessing.
- It books appointments into the calendar in the Dawnpipe app.
- It won't double-book: it checks the diary before offering a time.
- It transfers urgent calls to the owner or an on-call number they set.
- It records and transcribes calls, and the owner can play them back in the app.
- It answers in Spanish when the caller speaks Spanish.
- It recognises a repeat caller by their number.
- It filters spam and robocalls, and those don't use the plan's minutes.

**Finding customers**
- It finds businesses matching the customer's description of who they sell to.
- It gets a verified email address where it can, and says when one is a guess.
- It writes a personal email for each one, in the customer's own voice.
- **Nothing sends until the owner approves it.**
- It paces sending and warms up a new domain to protect deliverability.

**Plans and terms**
- Front Desk is $499/month and includes 500 talk-minutes and 200 leads.
- Other plans are $99, $299, $699 and $1,499 — read them off the site.
- Every plan has a 7-day free trial. A card is required; nothing is charged
  until day 8; cancel in one click before then and pay nothing.
- Month to month. No contract, no setup fee, no minimum term.
- **No overage charges, ever** — when an allowance runs out it pauses.
- Cancel any time; it runs to the end of the paid month.
- Their data is theirs and they keep it when they change plans.

## ❌ You may NOT say any of these

**Because it isn't true or isn't live**

- ❌ **Anything about text messages to their customers.** Booking confirmation
  texts, missed-call text-backs and emergency alert texts are built but **not
  switched on** pending carrier registration. Do not mention, promise, or
  demo them. *(Approved answer if asked directly is in the Rep Brief.)*
- ❌ **Anything about AI cold calling or auto-dialing for the customer.** We do
  not sell it. An AI voice calling someone who never asked is illegal under
  the TCPA at $500–$1,500 per call. Dawnpipe's outbound feature only calls
  people who requested a call and gave consent.
- ❌ It syncs with Google Calendar / Outlook / their existing CRM out of the box.
- ❌ Any specific competitor comparison you have not personally verified this month.

**Because we have no evidence for it**

- ❌ Any customer count, or "we have X customers", or "businesses like yours already use it."
- ❌ Any testimonial, review, case study, logo, or "trusted by."
- ❌ Any ROI figure, percentage, or dollar amount stated as what they **will** get
  — "this will make you $X" or "you'll book 30% more."
  *You may ask them what a job is worth and let them do their own arithmetic
  out loud. That is a question, not a claim.*
- ❌ Any uptime, accuracy, or answer-rate percentage.
- ❌ Any claim about how many calls they are currently missing, stated as fact
  about their business. Ask; don't assert.

**Because it's someone else's decision**

- ❌ Any discount, free month, price other than list, or "I can get you a deal."
- ❌ Any promise about a future feature or a date one will ship.
- ❌ Any commitment about support response time beyond "a real person answers
  support@dawnpipe.com."
- ❌ Anything about refunds beyond the published policy.

## How to handle "who else uses this?"

The honest answer, which converts better than a dodge:

> "You'd be one of the first — we're new. That's exactly why there's a free
> week with nothing charged, why you've got my direct number, and why you can
> call the AI right now and judge it yourself instead of taking my word."

**Never invent a customer, a name, or a story.** One fabricated reference is
worth less than every deal you will close this year, and it is immediate
termination for cause with forfeiture of all future commission.
