# Sell the thing that answers the phone when nobody else can

Dawnpipe is an AI receptionist for small service businesses. It picks up when
the owner is under a sink, on a roof, or driving between jobs. It asks what's
wrong, answers from what the owner told it, and books the job straight into
their calendar. It works nights, weekends and holidays, and it tells every
caller it's an AI.

We're building an independent sales network to put it in front of the people
who need it most: one-to-fifteen-person trades and service businesses where
the owner *is* the front desk.

## Why this sells

Every small contractor knows the number that hurts. A missed call is a job
that went to whoever picked up. In the trades, one job is worth several
hundred to several thousand dollars — and most owners are missing a quarter
to a third of their calls, all day, every day, because they're working.

You are not selling software. You are selling the four calls a week they
didn't know they were losing.

**And your prospect can hear it in ninety seconds.** Have them call our AI
line while you're on the phone with them. No slide deck, no trial signup, no
"let me get you with an engineer." They talk to it, and then they either want
it or they don't. That is a very unusual thing to have in a sales bag.

## The money

Commission is **30% of what Dawnpipe collects** from every account you sign,
every month, for that account's first 24 months. From month 25 it steps to
**10%** and continues for as long as the account is active and you are.

| Plan | Price | You earn, monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99 | $29.70 |
| Growth | $299 | $89.70 |
| **Front Desk** *(most sell this)* | **$499** | **$149.70** |
| Scale | $699 | $209.70 |
| Complete | $1,499 | $449.70 |

Front Desk is what a single-location trade buys: the AI answers their phone
with 500 talk-minutes a month, and between calls it finds them 200 new
customers and writes each one a personal email the owner approves before it
sends.

**How you get paid.** Commission accrues on money we actually collect, not on
signups. Nothing accrues during a customer's 7-day free trial, so your first
commission on a new account lands after their first real payment. Payouts go
out monthly. Refunds and chargebacks reverse the commission they came from —
you keep what the customer kept.

**Accounts stay yours.** Once an account is attributed to you it is yours; it
doesn't get reassigned because someone else spoke to them later.

**We are not going to pretend we have earnings data.** We don't. See the
disclosure at the bottom — read it.

## How attribution works, so you never have to chase a paycheck

Three ways a sale gets tracked to you, and you only need one of them:

1. **Your link.** You get `dawnpipe.com/r/YOURNAME`. Anyone who clicks it is
   tied to you for 90 days, even if they sign up next week from a different
   browser session.
2. **Your code, at checkout.** Your code is your last name. There's a
   "Referral code" box on the payment page. A customer who heard "use code
   SKINNER" and typed it is yours.
3. **You closed them by phone and they used neither.** Tell us; we assign the
   account to you and back-pay every invoice already collected from them.

You can see every account attributed to you and every dollar it has earned.

## What we give you

- Your referral link and code, issued the day your paperwork is in.
- A live demo line you can hand to any prospect, any time.
- A one-page brief: who to call, the opening line, the three objections you
  will hear, and what to say to each.
- A free Dawnpipe account of your own — use the product, and use its lead
  finder to build your own prospect list.
- A named human (the founder) who answers your questions the same day.

## Who this is for

You've sold to small business owners before. You know how to get an owner on
the phone at 7:40am or 4:30pm, and you know that "I'll think about it" means
you didn't make it real. You can run your own week without being managed.

You are comfortable being early. There are no customer logos, no case
studies, and no reference calls yet, because there are no customers yet. The
first reps here get the pick of the market and the accounts that come with it,
and in exchange they're selling something unproven. If that trade sounds bad
to you, this isn't the one.

## Who this is not for

Anyone who wants a base, a lead list handed over, or a manager telling them
what to do each morning. Anyone who wants to blast an autodialer or an AI
voice at strangers — that is illegal for this product and grounds for
immediate termination (see the compliance policy you'll sign).
