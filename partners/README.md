# Rep hiring package

Everything needed to recruit and run commission-only sales representatives.

| File | What it is | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| `JOB-AD-short.md` | Job-board ad, written to survive Indeed/Craigslist review | Candidates |
| `JOB-AD-long.md` | Full version for dawnpipe.com/partners | Candidates who clicked |
| `REP-BRIEF.md` | The one page a working rep keeps by the phone | Signed reps |
| `AGREEMENT.md` | Representative agreement + Exhibit A commission schedule | Signed reps |
| `APPROVED-CLAIMS.md` | Exactly what a rep may and may not say | Signed reps |

Everything here matches the code as of build `ec5f92a`: **30% for 24 months
then 10%, no activation bonus**, Front Desk at **$499** paying **$149.70/month**,
attribution by link *or* last-name code at Stripe checkout, and owner
assignment with back-pay for phone closes.

## Order of operations

**Do not post an ad until steps 1–3 are done.** A rep who signs up to a
half-built program quits in week two and tells other reps.

1. **Lawyer reviews `AGREEMENT.md`.** It's a complete draft, not a blank page,
   so this should be a cheap review rather than a drafting job. Two things to
   raise: multi-state sales-rep commission statutes (Texas/Washington/Florida
   have teeth and an Illinois choice-of-law clause won't displace them), and
   1099 classification.
2. **Form the LLC and get the EIN.** The agreement needs a legal entity to be
   a party to. This is also the gate on A2P 10DLC, so it unblocks texting too.
3. **Sanity-check the funnel yourself.** Call the demo line from a phone that
   isn't yours. Sign up as a customer with a rep code. Confirm the account
   shows up attributed in the Sales Reps tab. Ten minutes; do it before anyone
   else does it for you.
4. **Post `JOB-AD-short.md`** to Time To Hire (~$399, purpose-built for
   commission-only) and one Craigslist metro (~$25). Put `JOB-AD-long.md` on
   dawnpipe.com/partners. Post the long version as an organic LinkedIn *Page*
   update — **never LinkedIn Jobs**, which prohibits commission-only postings
   outright.
5. **Onboard each rep in this exact order:** agreement signed → claims sheet
   signed → W-9 received → payout method → *then* issue the code. The app
   already blocks payout until the first four are ticked; keeping the code
   back until then is the operational half of the same control.

## The honest risks

**Reps quit commission-only roles at 30–60 days** when the money hasn't
arrived. With no signing bonus, the first payment on a new account lands
roughly 35–45 days after the customer starts paying — so realistically 6–8
weeks from a rep's first close. Say that number out loud in the intro call.
A rep who hears it up front and stays is worth three who feel misled.

**Nothing here works without proof.** The demo line is your only real asset,
and it's a good one — a prospect hearing the product beats any deck. Make sure
it answers reliably before a rep points anyone at it.

**Texting is off.** Until 10DLC registration completes, reps must not sell it.
That's written into the brief and the claims sheet. Re-issue both when it
goes live — and tell every rep, because they'll want to lead with it.
