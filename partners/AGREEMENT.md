# Independent Sales Representative Agreement — DRAFT

> **⚠ Have a lawyer review this before a single rep signs it.**
>
> This is a working draft written to be *complete and specific* so an attorney
> is editing rather than starting from scratch — which is cheaper and faster
> than handing them a blank page. It is not legal advice.
>
> Two things to raise with counsel specifically:
> 1. **Sales-rep commission statutes.** Around 35 states have them, and several
>    (Texas, Washington, Florida) require a written agreement stating how
>    commission is computed and paid, with treble damages and fee-shifting for
>    unpaid commissions after termination. An Illinois choice-of-law clause
>    will **not** reliably displace another state's protective statute.
> 2. **Worker classification.** Illinois' three-part test (820 ILCS 115/2) and
>    the current federal test both matter. Note that phone and internet sales
>    do **not** qualify for the outside-sales exemption (29 CFR 541.502).

**This Agreement** is made between **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, an Illinois
limited liability company ("Dawnpipe"), and **[REP LEGAL NAME]**
("Representative"), effective **[DATE]**.

## 1. What the Representative does

1.1 Representative will market and solicit orders for Dawnpipe's subscription
software services to prospective customers in the United States.

1.2 Representative is **not** granted a territory, an exclusive market, an
account list, or a quota. Dawnpipe and other representatives may solicit the
same prospects.

1.3 Representative has **no authority to bind Dawnpipe**, to accept orders, to
set or vary prices, to grant discounts or refunds, to modify these or any
customer terms, to make commitments about future features, or to hold funds.
All orders are accepted or rejected by Dawnpipe in its sole discretion, and
all customers contract directly with Dawnpipe.

1.4 Representative pays nothing to Dawnpipe. There is no fee, purchase,
inventory, deposit, training charge, or minimum, at any time.

## 2. Independent contractor

2.1 Representative is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner,
agent for any purpose beyond soliciting orders, or joint venturer.

2.2 Representative controls their own schedule, methods, and place of work,
provides their own equipment, and may work for others including others in the
same industry.

2.3 Representative receives **no** base salary, draw, wage, expense
reimbursement, benefits, insurance, or paid leave, and is responsible for all
federal, state and local taxes on amounts received. A completed **IRS Form
W-9 must be on file before any payment**; Dawnpipe issues Form 1099-NEC where
required.

## 3. Commission

3.1 Dawnpipe pays commission per the **Commission Schedule (Exhibit A)**,
which forms part of this Agreement.

3.2 Commission is earned only on **subscription revenue Dawnpipe actually
collects** from an Attributed Account, calculated net of refunds, chargebacks,
failed payments, discounts, promotional credits, and sales or other taxes. No
commission accrues on trials, unpaid invoices, one-time or set-up fees, or
third-party pass-through charges.

3.3 **Attribution.** An account is attributed to Representative when it is
recorded to Representative's referral code or link at signup or checkout, or
when Dawnpipe assigns it to Representative in writing. **First attribution
controls.** An attributed account is not reassigned except by Dawnpipe in
writing, with a stated reason, and reassignment does not move commission
already accrued.

3.4 **Reversal.** If Dawnpipe refunds, credits, or loses to chargeback any
amount on which commission was paid or accrued, the corresponding commission
is reversed and offset against amounts otherwise payable. A negative balance
carries forward against future commission only; **Dawnpipe will not invoice
Representative for a negative balance.**

3.5 **Survival on departure.** If this Agreement ends other than for cause
under §7.2, commission continues to be paid on each Attributed Account at the
introductory rate for the remainder of that account's 24-month introductory
period, whether or not Representative sells again. The maintenance rate under
Exhibit A does not survive termination.

3.6 Dawnpipe may change the Commission Schedule for **accounts attributed
after** the change on 30 days' written notice. Rates on already-attributed
accounts do not change.

## 4. How the Representative may sell

4.1 Representative will make only statements set out in the **Approved Claims
Sheet**, which Dawnpipe may update in writing. Representative will make **no**
earnings claim, income projection, ROI figure, customer count, testimonial,
case study, or performance guarantee about Dawnpipe or its services.

4.2 Representative will comply with the **Outreach Compliance Policy**,
including: no autodialer, artificial or prerecorded voice, or AI voice in
Representative's own prospecting; Do-Not-Call scrubbing and internal
do-not-call list; lawful calling hours; and CAN-SPAM compliance for email.

4.3 Representative will not: represent themselves as an employee or officer of
Dawnpipe; register any domain, advertising account, social handle, or business
name containing "Dawnpipe" or a confusingly similar mark; bid on the Dawnpipe
mark in paid search; or offer any inducement, rebate, or discount not
authorised in writing.

4.4 **Indemnity.** Representative will indemnify Dawnpipe against claims,
penalties and defence costs arising from Representative's breach of §4,
including any claim under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, state
telemarketing law, or consumer protection law arising from Representative's
own outreach conduct.

## 5. Confidentiality and materials

5.1 Representative will keep Dawnpipe's non-public information confidential and
will not use customer or prospect data obtained through Dawnpipe for any
purpose other than selling Dawnpipe.

5.2 Dawnpipe grants a limited, revocable, non-exclusive licence to use
Dawnpipe-supplied names, marks and materials solely to sell Dawnpipe, ending
when this Agreement ends.

## 6. No solicitation of Dawnpipe's customers

6.1 During this Agreement and for 12 months after, Representative will not
solicit any Dawnpipe customer to switch to a competing AI receptionist or
outbound-messaging product. Nothing here restricts Representative from working
in the industry generally or from selling non-competing products at any time.

## 7. Term and termination

7.1 Either party may terminate on 14 days' written notice, for any reason.

7.2 Dawnpipe may terminate **immediately for cause** for: any material breach
of §4; misrepresenting the product to a prospect or customer; any autodialer,
AI-voice, prerecorded-message, or do-not-call violation; self-referral or
manipulation of attribution; fraud; or conduct exposing Dawnpipe to legal
claims. **Termination for cause forfeits all future commission, including the
§3.5 tail.** Amounts already paid are not recovered except for fraud.

7.3 §§2.3, 3.4, 3.5, 4.4, 5, 6 and 8 survive.

## 8. General

8.1 This Agreement, the Commission Schedule, the Approved Claims Sheet and the
Outreach Compliance Policy are the entire agreement, and supersede any advert,
web page, or conversation — including any figure appearing in recruiting
material.

8.2 Governed by Illinois law, without regard to conflict of laws, **except**
where the law of the state in which Representative works provides
non-waivable protections, which apply to that extent.

8.3 No assignment by Representative without written consent. Amendments in
writing. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest stands.

8.4 **No guarantee of income.** Representative acknowledges that Dawnpipe has
made no representation about earnings; that Dawnpipe has no earnings data to
provide; that income depends entirely on Representative's own results; and
that Representative may earn nothing.

# Exhibit A — Commission Schedule

*Effective [DATE]. Applies to accounts attributed on or after this date.*

## Rate

| When | Rate on collected revenue |
|---|---|
| An account's billing months **1–24** | **30%** |
| Month **25 onward** | **10%**, while both the account and the Representative are active |

"Active" for a Representative means at least one new account reached its first
successful payment in the preceding 12 months.

**There is no signing or activation bonus.**

## What it pays on, per current plans

| Plan | Price / month | Commission months 1–24 | Month 25+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99 | $29.70 | $9.90 |
| Growth | $299 | $89.70 | $29.90 |
| **Front Desk** | **$499** | **$149.70** | $49.90 |
| Scale | $699 | $209.70 | $69.90 |
| Complete | $1,499 | $449.70 | $149.90 |

Prices may change; commission always follows the amount actually collected. If
a customer upgrades or downgrades, commission follows the new amount from the
next invoice, and the account stays with the same Representative.

## Basis

Commission is calculated on **collected subscription revenue**: the amount
Dawnpipe receives on a paid invoice, **less** sales and other taxes, refunds,
credits, discounts and chargebacks. It excludes trials, unpaid or failed
invoices, one-time fees, and third-party pass-through.

## Timing

- **Nothing accrues during a customer's 7-day free trial** — a trial collects
  no money.
- Commission accrues when an invoice is **collected**.
- Amounts accrued in a calendar month are paid on or about the **15th of the
  following month**.
- An account's **first** collected invoice is subject to a **30-day hold**
  before it becomes payable, so a fast refund or chargeback nets out before
  money moves. In practice, expect the first payment on a new account roughly
  **35–45 days** after it starts paying.
- Payment requires a signed Agreement, a W-9 on file, and a payout method.

## Reversal and netting

A refund, credit or chargeback reverses the commission on the affected amount,
proportionally for a partial refund. Reversals net against the next payout. A
negative balance carries forward against future commission only and is never
invoiced.

## Cancelled and returning accounts

Commission stops at the end of the last period actually collected. If a
cancelled account re-subscribes **within 90 days**, it returns to the original
Representative at its original month count. After 90 days it is a house
account and earns no commission.

## Where to check it

Every accrual, hold, reversal and payment is recorded as a line in Dawnpipe's
commission ledger with the Stripe invoice it came from. Representative may
request their ledger at any time; Dawnpipe will provide it within 5 business
days.
