# Competitor research — 2026-11-16

**No pricing changes among the 11 tracked competitors, but two real findings: Zoom now has a live official pricing page (ending a 10+ week gap), and ElevenLabs quietly launched a direct competitor, Reception.ai.**

## Pricing — unchanged
Rosie, Dialzara, Smith.ai (AI Receptionist line, confirmed via smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist), Ruby, Sameday, Beside Phone + Beside Agent, Phonely (hero-card-vs-feature-table minute discrepancy still present, unchanged), Newo.ai, RingCentral AIR ($49 standalone / $39+$30 bundle, confirmed on the live US page), and Upfirst — all re-verified directly on official pricing pages this week, no changes from 2026-11-09.

**Zoom** — now has a genuine official standalone pricing page (zoom.com/.../ai-receptionist), after 10+ weeks of no official page being findable. Confirmed: $25/mo billed annually or $29.99/mo monthly, 100 min — matches last week's secondary-source estimate almost exactly, so no real pricing move, just a data-gap closed. Also newly documented: a Zoom Phone + Virtual Agent bundle at $0.25/min + Zoom Phone cost, not previously tracked.

## New entrant
**ElevenLabs launched "Reception by ElevenAgents"** (reception.ai) — a packaged, self-serve AI receptionist from a major voice-AI company. Basic $29/mo (75 min, $0.45/min overage), Plus $79/mo (275 min), Premium $199/mo (1,000 min, $0.30/min overage, multi-location). Semrush shows negligible traffic so far (16 organic visits/mo vs. Rosie's 2,920), so it's early-stage, but ElevenLabs' brand and voice-tech reach make it worth tracking going forward. Also noted ThunderPhone (thunderphone.com) — a developer/API voice infra platform (2¢/min, OpenAI-Realtime-compatible), not a packaged SMB product, so not adding it to the tracked list.
