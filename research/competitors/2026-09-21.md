## New entrant: Phonely
YC-backed AI voice/omnichannel receptionist, just raised a $22M Series A (Base10 Partners, ~$100M valuation) — not previously tracked. Pricing: Free (100 min), Starter $50/mo (250 min, $33 annual), Pro $150/mo (750 min, $100 annual, adds outbound + SIP trunking + A/B call-script testing), Enterprise from $0.05/min. Overage $0.25/min; post-transfer-to-human minutes $0.02/min. Omnichannel (voice/SMS/chat/API), unlimited concurrent calls on paid tiers. Semrush: rank 673,623, ~2,059 visits — already has real traffic despite being new to our tracking.

## Intermedia AI Receptionist — no update
Still open beta, GA still slated for October 2026, pricing still not public. One-line watch item until GA.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Ruby's across-the-board price increase validates COST.md's own $399→$449 move** — the market's highest-profile incumbent just raised prices on every tier while adding nothing new; this is a good week to make that change, not a risk.
2. **Phonely is the new low-end/omnichannel threat to watch, not Beside.** Its free tier plus $50 entry plan undercut Front Desk on price, and its per-minute overage model (vs. Dawnpipe's flat no-overage allowance) is a concrete objection-handling contrast worth having ready.
3. **Sameday's traffic drop is a single data point** — recommend just re-checking next week before drawing conclusions; Semrush's monthly refresh cycle makes one week of data unreliable on its own (same caveat as Rosie/Smith.ai in prior reports).
