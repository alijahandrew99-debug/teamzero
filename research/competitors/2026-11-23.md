# Competitor research — 2026-11-23

**No pricing changes across all 12 tracked competitors, but a real new entrant this week: Pie's "AI Front Desk" (VC-backed, Lightspeed-led) — and it shares Dawnpipe's own plan name. Ruby's paid-search exit has gone from pullback to near-total collapse.**

## Pricing — unchanged
Rosie, Dialzara, Smith.ai, Ruby, Sameday, Beside (Phone + Agent), Phonely, Newo.ai, Zoom, RingCentral AIR, Upfirst — all re-verified directly on official pricing pages this week via Firecrawl, no changes from 2026-11-16. **Reception.ai** (first full tracked week) has slightly *lower* confirmed overage rates than last week's discovery estimate: Basic $0.40/min, Plus $0.32/min, Premium $0.22/min (was est. $0.45/$0.30) — a data correction, not a mid-week price cut.

## Notable signals (no price change, but worth flagging)
- **Ruby's paid-search exit is now near-total**: Adwords keywords 3,259 (Aug) → 17 this week; spend cratered to $26,707 (was $103k in Sep). Fourth consecutive report tracking this decline — Ruby appears to be vacating paid search entirely, not just pulling back.
- **Phonely shipped its own in-house voice model, "Alma"** — claims 182ms latency vs. 490ms for GPT-4.1, pricing unchanged. First real "faster AI" marketing claim seen among tracked competitors.
- Reception.ai traffic growing fast off a tiny base (16 → 205 organic visits/mo) — still negligible vs. Rosie's 3,202/mo.

## New entrant
**Pie — "AI Front Desk" (getpie.com)**, from Pie Tech Inc. (ex-Toast founders, NYC). Raised a $19.5–23.7M Series A led by Lightspeed with American Express Ventures and Capital One, publicly launched June 2026. Packaged 24/7 AI phone receptionist (reservations, FAQs, custom voice). No public pricing page — sales-assisted/demo-gated; third-party press reports ~$359/mo for a single location, first month free, $300 signup credit. **Notable: Pie's product is also named "Front Desk," same as Dawnpipe's own plan.** Source: getpie.com, getpie.com/ai-front-desk, corroborated via SiliconAngle/PYMNTS/BusinessWire funding coverage.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Watch for "Front Desk" name collision in search/SEO.** Pie is VC-backed and will spend on marketing; if their "AI Front Desk" starts ranking for queries Dawnpipe wants ("AI front desk pricing," etc.), it's worth a brand/SEO check — not an action to take now, just a trend to monitor before it compounds.
2. **Ruby's near-total paid-search exit (3,259→17 keywords) is a four-week-confirmed trend, not noise.** If Alijah wants to run paid search, Ruby's historical keyword set is likely cheap/uncontested right now — a concrete, low-risk paid-acquisition opening.
3. **Phonely's latency-focused "Alma" launch is the first competitor marketing raw response speed.** Worth a look at whether Dawnpipe's own avg. turn latency is competitive enough to make the same claim, or whether this becomes a feature gap worth closing.
