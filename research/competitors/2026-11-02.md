## New entrant — Upfirst (upfirst.ai)
Not previously tracked. Per-call answering service, four self-serve tiers + custom, confirmed on upfirst.ai/pricing: **$24.95/mo (30 calls, $1.50/extra call) → $59.95/mo (90 calls, $1.00/extra) → $159.95/mo (300 calls, $0.75/extra) → $299/mo (600 calls, $0.70/extra) → Custom (volume pricing, BAAs)**. 20% off on annual billing. Features include 35+ languages with auto-translation, call memory for returning callers, and an extra-agent add-on at $9.95/mo for multi-location setups. No lead-gen/email component. Positioned similarly to Rosie/Dialzara on price but billed per-call like Smith.ai.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Upfirst's per-call structure makes direct minute comparison hard, but at typical call lengths (~2-3 min) its $299/600-call tier lands around $0.50–$0.75 effective per minute** — notably more expensive than Rosie or Dialzara at comparable volume despite a similar-looking entry price ($24.95). Worth having this math ready if Upfirst comes up in sales conversations, since its sticker price looks cheaper than it is at real volume.
2. **No pricing competitor currently charges less than Front Desk for both voice minutes and lead-gen bundled together** — this gap has now held unchanged for 10 consecutive weeks (since 2026-08-31) across 11 tracked competitors. Confidence in this positioning claim is now high; it's a safe, durable line for marketing copy rather than one that needs week-to-week hedging.
