# Competitor research — 2026-09-14

**Two new entrants dominate this week; core five held pricing steady.**
Intermedia (major UCaaS incumbent) launched an AI Receptionist into open beta,
and Beside — a $32M-funded voice startup — surfaced with a sub-$100/mo
product. No pricing-page changes at Rosie, Ruby, or Sameday since last check.

## Rosie — heyrosie.com/pricing
No pricing change: $49/250min → $149/1,000min → $299/2,000min, unchanged.
Semrush: rank 463,387 (was 340,490), ~3,315 mo organic visits (was ~4,832) —
a real drop if the index has refreshed, but Semrush's organic data updates
monthly, so treat as a signal to recheck next week rather than confirmed.

## Dialzara — dialzara.com/pricing
Core tiers unchanged ($29/$99/$199/$349). **New since last check:** a
white-label/partner pricing section — agencies can resell the platform under
their own brand with multi-tenant dashboards and custom per-partner bundling
of inbound/outbound/SMS/chat (dialzara.com/faqs). Outbound confirmed at
$750/mo for 1,500 contacts (contact-based, not per-minute). Semrush: rank
878,095 (was 922,891), ~1,420 visits (was ~1,319) — slight improvement.

## Sameday — sameday.ai/pricing
No change: Launch $449/500min → Scale $789/1,000min, still no lead/email

## New entrant: Intermedia AI Receptionist
Intermedia (established UCaaS provider) launched a native AI Receptionist
into **open beta** on 2026-09-10, GA expected October 2026. Configured via
their existing "AI Builder," it plugs into Intermedia Unite. Pricing not yet
public. This is the second platform incumbent to enter (after Zoom in July)
— distribution neither Rosie/Dialzara/Smith.ai/Ruby/Sameday has. Source:
globenewswire.com/telecomreseller.com, 2026-09-10/11.

## New entrant: Beside
Well-funded ($32M total, $20M Series A led by EQT Ventures, Nov 2025 — not
new money, but new to our tracking) AI receptionist for small business, with
its own ground-up telephony stack (not layered on existing phone systems).
Pricing: **Baseline $29.99/mo (30 AI-answered calls)**, **Unlimited $99.99/mo
(no call cap)** — undercuts the entire named set by 4–10x on entry price.
Includes transcripts, appointment booking, and multi-seat team features, no
lead-gen/outbound product. Source: beside.com/pricing.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Sharpen the bundle pitch now.** Two new entrants this week (Intermedia's
   distribution, Beside's price) both compete on voice alone with no lead-gen
   attached — the "voice + outbound leads in one price" gap Dawnpipe sits in
   just got more crowded from both the low end and the platform-incumbent
   end simultaneously.
2. **Beside's $29.99/$99.99 tiers reset the low-end anchor** (below even
   Zoom's $29.99/100min from July) — worth knowing this number exists when
   objection-handling on price, even though Front Desk doesn't compete at
   that tier.
3. **Stop chasing the Smith.ai annual tier.** Two straight weeks unconfirmed
   on smith.ai itself despite direct access this week — likely a
   loman.ai/schedulingkit.com misread of a different Smith.ai product line.
   Deprioritize unless it appears on smith.ai directly.
