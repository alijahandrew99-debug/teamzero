## Beside — beside.com/pricing
**Major repositioning, confirmed directly on-site.** Old plans (Baseline $29.99/mo/30 calls, Unlimited $99.99/mo uncapped, +$4.99/line) are gone. New: **Part-time $99/mo (5,000 credits)** and **Full-time $249.99/mo (12,500 credits)** — a credit-based model for a broader "agent" that does calls plus website/SEO work, review management, and follow-ups, not just call answering. Pure phone-answering pricing has moved to a separate page (beside.com/phone/pricing, not yet checked). This is a ~3–8x list-price increase and a shift out of the low-end receptionist category entirely.
Semrush: rank 505,038 (was 606,638), ~3,004 visits (was ~2,365) — up despite (or ahead of) the repricing.

## New entrant: Zoom Virtual Agent Receptionist
Zoom launched a **standalone** AI receptionist in July 2026 that works with any phone system (no Zoom Phone required) — news.zoom.com confirms the announcement. **$29.99/mo for 100 minutes ($24.99/mo annual)**, 10+ languages, live transcription, scheduling, call routing. Not previously tracked; a tech giant entering at the entry-price tier with existing SMB brand trust and distribution is a bigger threat than Phonely's YC-backed entry.

## Phonely — phonely.ai/pricing
No pricing change (Free 100min → Starter $50/250min → Pro $150/750min → Enterprise ~$0.05/min), confirmed on-site — note the page itself shows conflicting minute counts between the hero cards (200/650) and the detailed feature table (250/750); treating the table as authoritative since it matches last week's figures. New: Phonely launched **"Alma,"** a proprietary voice model it claims hits 182ms time-to-first-token vs. 490ms for GPT-4.1 — a latency/cost differentiation play worth knowing about even though it doesn't change pricing.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Beside just vacated the low end.** Its jump from ~$30–100/mo call-based tiers to $99–$250/mo credit-based "agent" pricing removes it as a cheap alternative and reframes it above Front Desk. That leaves Rosie/Dialzara as Front Desk's only cheaper competitors — worth a line in marketing copy contrasting "a focused receptionist + lead-gen product" against Beside's broader, pricier, less-defined "agent."
2. **Zoom, not Phonely, is this week's real pricing threat.** $29.99/mo undercuts Front Desk badly, but it caps at 100 minutes and has zero lead-gen. A ready objection-handler: "Zoom answers your phone; Dawnpipe answers your phone *and* fills your pipeline with 200 qualified leads a month" — worth having before a prospect brings it up.
3. **Ruby's continuing paid-search pullback (328→250 Adwords keywords in two weeks)** while its list prices just rose is a signal it's leaning harder on its incumbent/organic position rather than buying growth — no action needed, just a trend worth tracking in case it signals margin pressure at the high end.
