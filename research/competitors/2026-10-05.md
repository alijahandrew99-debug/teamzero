## Beside — beside.com (two products, both confirmed on-site)
**Resolves last week's open item.** The separate Phone pricing page is live: **Baseline $29/mo (30 AI Receptionist calls, 1 line)** and **Unlimited $99/mo (uncapped AI Receptionist calls)** — essentially the same as Beside's pre-repositioning plans (previously $29.99/$99.99), just rounded down ~$1. So last week's read ("Beside vacated the low end") was incomplete: Beside now runs **two parallel pricing pages** — the cheap, call-count-based Phone product, and the separate credit-based Agent workspace (Part-time $99/mo/5,000 credits, Full-time $249.99/mo/12,500 credits, confirmed unchanged). Beside still has a sub-$50 entry point; it just lives on a different URL than before.
Semrush: rank 545,393 (was 505,038), ~2,700 visits (was ~3,004) — down.

Also offers a **Zoom Phone + AI receptionist bundle from $0.25/min billed annually** (plus Zoom Phone cost) as a usage-based alternative to the flat tier — not previously noted, worth knowing as a second pricing path into the same product.

## Intermedia AI Receptionist — still waitlist-only
No change from last week: the product page's only CTA remains "Join the waitlist," no public pricing. Confirms last week's read — treat as beta-with-a-launch-headline, not GA.

## New entrant: Newo.ai
Well-funded ($25M Series A Feb 2026, $32M total) multi-channel AI receptionist (voice, SMS, web chat, social), confirmed via newo.ai/pricing. **Unit-metered pricing**: Pay-As-You-Go ($0 to start, 50 free units once, $1.75/unit after), **AI Receptionist $299/mo (200 units included, $1.50/unit additional)**, Enterprise custom. A "unit" ≈ one voice call up to 3 minutes, or ~30 text messages — a genuinely different pricing abstraction than everyone else's per-minute or per-call model. Semrush: rank 1,295,138, ~821 visits, 1,761 organic keywords — small today but worth a baseline given the funding.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Newo's $299/mo undercuts Front Desk by $100 but sells "units," not minutes** — a voice call under 3 minutes costs 1 unit regardless of length, text costs fractional units, and nothing in its pricing resembles a lead-gen product. "1,000 minutes and 200 leads, no unit math" is a clean, concrete contrast if Newo comes up in a sales conversation.
2. **Ruby's AdWords pullback is no longer a one-off** — four straight weeks of decline (332→328→250→235, ~29% total) while list prices are up. Worth tracking as a signal of margin pressure or a strategic pivot to organic/incumbent reliance at the high end; no pricing action needed yet, but flag if it continues past 6 weeks.
3. **Beside's Phone product means the sub-$50 entry tier didn't disappear** — correcting last week's take. Its 30-call cap on the $29 Baseline plan is stingy next to Front Desk's 1,000 minutes, so the contrast still favors Dawnpipe, but the "Beside vacated the low end" framing shouldn't go into marketing copy as written.
