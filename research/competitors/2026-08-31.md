# Competitor research — 2026-08-31

**First baseline report.** No prior week to diff against — numbers below are the
starting point for future weeks. Headline: Zoom launched a standalone AI phone
receptionist in July at $29.99/mo, undercutting the whole named set on price.

## Rosie — heyrosie.com/pricing
$49/mo (250 min, message-taking only) → $149/mo (1,000 min, booking + live
transfer) → $299/mo (2,000 min, waterfall transfer, custom training). No setup
fee, 7-day trial, no published overage rate on the pricing page itself.
Semrush: rank 343,405, ~4,787 mo organic visits, 1,697 keywords.

## Dialzara — dialzara.com/pricing
Business Lite $29/mo (60 min) → Pro $99/mo (220 min) → Plus $199/mo (500 min)
→ Elite $349/mo (1,000 min). Overage scales down by tier, $0.48/min → $0.35/min.
No setup fee. Semrush: rank 938,520, ~1,286 mo organic visits, 2,918 keywords —
smallest traffic footprint of the five.

## Smith.ai — smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist
New since any prior knowledge: a **$0/mo Free tier** (25 calls, $3/call after)
now sits below the $150/$270/$500/$800 paid tiers (300–2,000+ calls,
$1.60–$2.00/call, $2.10–$3.00 overage). Per-call, not per-minute. Semrush: rank
105,917, ~18,365 mo organic visits, 15,010 keywords — largest traffic and SEO
footprint of the five by a wide margin.

## Ruby — ruby.com/plans-and-pricing
$245/mo (50 min) → $385/mo (100 min) → $1,695/mo (500 min), no setup/holiday/
transfer fees. Effective ~$3.40–$4.90/min, well above the AI-native players.
Semrush: rank 158,051, ~11,786 mo organic visits, 4,136 keywords, and the only
one of the five spending meaningfully on paid search (3,259 Adwords keywords,
~$148.7k organic-cost-equivalent) — Ruby is buying its way to visibility, not
winning it organically.

## Sameday — sameday.ai/pricing
Launch $449/mo (500 min, 3 locations) → Scale $789/mo (1,000 min, unlimited
locations, voice cloning); no lead/email product at any tier. Semrush: rank
2,193,941, ~322 mo organic visits, 87 keywords — negligible organic presence
despite the home-services vertical push (ServiceTitan integration).

## New entrant: Zoom Virtual Agent Receptionist
Zoom announced (July 9, 2026) a **standalone** AI receptionist that plugs into
any existing phone system, not just Zoom Phone — $29.99/mo for 100 min
($24.99/mo annual), 10+ languages, scheduling, routing. A well-capitalized
platform incumbent now competes directly at the bottom of this market with
distribution none of the five named competitors have.

## Recommendations (proposals only)

1. **Don't chase Zoom on price.** Front Desk can't win a per-minute price war
   against a platform giant subsidized by its core product. The defensible
   angle is the bundle: none of Rosie/Dialzara/Smith.ai/Ruby/Sameday bundle
   outbound lead-gen with voice — Dawnpipe is alone on that axis. Worth
   sharpening in positioning copy rather than reacting on price.
2. **A "what you'd actually pay elsewhere" comparison could land well.** All
   five competitors charge $99–$349/mo (or $0.35–$5/min-equivalent) for voice
   alone, then leads would be a separate purchase entirely. That gap is
   concrete and citable if Alijah wants a pricing-page comparison table.
3. **Watch, don't act:** Smith.ai's new $0/mo/25-call free tier is a
   downward-pricing signal at the low end of the market. Not a threat to
   Front Desk's segment today, but worth re-checking next month to see if it
   sticks or if others follow.
