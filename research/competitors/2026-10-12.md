# Competitor research — 2026-10-12

**No pricing changes among Rosie, Dialzara, Smith.ai, Ruby, Sameday, Beside, Phonely, Newo, or Zoom this week — all confirmed directly on-site. Three non-pricing developments instead: Phonely launched an in-house voice model marketed on latency, Ruby is now surfacing AI features on its human-receptionist plans, and Semrush's traffic_overview tool lost MCP access (see data note).**

## Rosie — heyrosie.com/pricing
No change: $49/250min → $149/1,000min → $299/2,000min. Website Chat and Website Texting add-ons ($50/mo, 25 conversations) unchanged.

## Dialzara — dialzara.com/pricing
No change: Business Lite/Pro/Plus/Elite $29/$99/$199/$349 (60/220/500/1,000 min), same overage rates ($0.48→$0.35/min) and outbound/SMS/chatbot add-ons as last week, confirmed directly on-site.

## Smith.ai — smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist
No change: Free ($0, 25 calls)/Pro ($150/mo, $2.00/call)/Enterprise ($500+/mo, $1.67/call), confirmed directly on-site.

## Ruby — ruby.com/plans-and-pricing
No pricing change: $250/50min → $395/100min → $720/200min → $1,725/500min, confirmed directly on-site. **Newly noticed:** the page now markets "optional AI enhancements... available in all plans, at no extra cost," listing AI-powered transcripts, sentiment analysis, and "AI-assistant-supported call flows" as standard features — a shift toward hybrid AI+human positioning, not previously called out in this tracker (may have been there longer than one week; this is the first time we looked closely). Separately, **Adwords Keywords fell again to 147** (was 235 last week; 250/328/332 the three weeks before that) — a sixth straight week of paid-search pullback, now down ~56% from the five-weeks-ago peak.

## Sameday — sameday.ai/pricing

## Beside — beside.com / beside.com/phone/pricing

## Phonely — phonely.ai/pricing
No pricing change (Free 100min → Starter $50/250min → Pro $150/750min → Enterprise ~$0.05/min floor); the hero-card-vs-feature-table minute discrepancy flagged in prior reports persists unchanged. **New:** Phonely launched "Alma," its own in-house voice model, marketed at 182ms time-to-first-token vs. 490ms for GPT-4.1 — a latency-focused positioning move, not a pricing change.

## Newo.ai — newo.ai/pricing
No change: Pay-As-You-Go ($0, 50 free units once) / AI Receptionist ($299/mo, 200 units, $1.50/unit additional) / Enterprise custom, confirmed directly on-site. Several third-party aggregators show different, stale-looking tiers ($99/$299/$499/$799) — the official page is the one to trust.

## Zoom Virtual Agent Receptionist — zoom.com
No change: $25/mo billed annually or $29.99/mo monthly (100 min), or $0.25/min billed annually bundled with Zoom Phone, confirmed directly on-site.

## New entrant scan
No new SMB-focused startup found this week. One previously-untracked big-platform entrant surfaced in this week's broader scan: **Cisco Webex AI Receptionist**, generally available since June 2026 (not new this week, just newly noticed) — an org-level add-on for Webex Calling customers, $100 per 250-minute bundle plus $115 per additional 250-minute bundle, enterprise/multi-location oriented (up to 100 pooled instances per org). Less relevant to Front Desk's single-location SMB buyer than Zoom's standalone offer, but confirms the "big platforms bundling AI receptionist into their existing phone system" trend now spans Zoom, Cisco, and (per last week) Intermedia-in-beta.

## Data note
Semrush's `traffic_overview` report (the source of prior weeks' "~X visits" figures) returned a `no_subscription` error this week — MCP access to that report has lapsed on the current plan. `domain_rank` was used instead, but it's a different methodology (keyword-based organic-traffic estimate, not visit counts) and isn't comparable to prior weeks' visit figures. Nearly every tracked domain's Semrush Rank moved in the same direction this week under `domain_rank`, which looks like a database-wide refresh rather than individual signal, so no rank-based conclusions are drawn this week — Ruby's Adwords Keywords count (a stable, comparable metric) is the one traffic-adjacent figure called out above.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Phonely's "Alma" launch validates the architecture bet in COST.md** — a competitor is now marketing raw voice-latency as the product, pointing the same direction as COST.md's Path D (own the audio path: Media Streams + Deepgram + Polly direct). Worth weighing as a timing argument for prioritizing that migration, on top of the existing cost case.
2. **Ruby is folding AI into its "100% live human" plans at no extra charge.** If a prospect mentions "Ruby does AI too," the accurate response is that Ruby's AI features sit on top of $250–$1,725/mo human-staffed plans, not a standalone AI-only option — Front Desk's $399 for 1,000 AI-only minutes is still the cheaper AI-native alternative by a wide margin.
3. **Ruby's AdWords pullback is now a sustained 6-week trend** (332→328→250→235→147, ~56% decline) while list prices are flat. No pricing action needed, but worth flagging to sales as a signal the high end may be leaning more on existing customers/referrals than paid acquisition — a possible opening for outbound targeting price-sensitive Ruby prospects.
