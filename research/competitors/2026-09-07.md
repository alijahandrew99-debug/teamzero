# Competitor research — 2026-09-07

**Real movement this week.** Dialzara unbundled SMS/chat/outbound into paid
add-ons, Smith.ai appears to have added a price-locked annual AI tier, Ruby's
paid-search footprint contracted sharply, and a live AI-disclosure lawsuit
raises a compliance question worth checking on Dawnpipe's own call flow.

## Rosie — heyrosie.com/pricing
No change: Professional $49/mo (250 min) → Scale $149/mo (1,000 min,
booking+transfer) → Growth $299/mo (2,000 min). Semrush: rank 340,490 (was
343,405), ~4,832 mo organic visits (was ~4,787) — flat.

## Dialzara — dialzara.com/pricing
Core tiers unchanged ($29/$99/$199/$349 for 60/220/500/1,000 min). **New
since last check:** SMS agent, web chatbot, and outbound calling are now sold
as separate paid add-ons (chatbot ~$39/mo standalone, free only if bundled
with a voice plan; outbound priced per-contact). Dialzara is shifting from a
flat inbound-AI price toward an omnichannel upsell model. Source:
dialzara.com/pricing + dialzara.com/blog (Dec 2025 update, first seen by us
this week). Semrush: rank 922,891 (was 938,520), ~1,319 visits — flat.

## Smith.ai — smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist
Base structure holds: $0/mo free (25 calls) → paid AI tiers up to $800/mo,
$1.60–$2.00/call. **Possible new tier:** secondary pricing summaries
(loman.ai, schedulingkit.com) describe an annual "Done-for-You AI" plan,
price-locked at $500/$1,000/$2,000/mo with no overage and a dedicated
success manager. Could not confirm on smith.ai directly — this environment's
egress to smith.ai is blocked and it didn't surface in Firecrawl search
results, so **treat as unconfirmed until re-checked next week.** Semrush:
rank 105,913, ~18,297 visits — flat, still the largest footprint of the five.

## Ruby — ruby.com/plans-and-pricing
Pricing unchanged: $245/50min → $385/100min → $1,695/500min. Notable:
Google Ads keyword count fell from 3,259 to 392 (~88%) since last check —
Ruby appears to have pulled back sharply on paid search spend. Semrush: rank
160,079 (was 158,051), ~11,588 visits.

## Sameday — sameday.ai/pricing
Pricing unchanged: Launch $449/500min → Scale $789/1,000min, still no
lead/email product. Organic traffic more than doubled (322 → 794 mo visits)
and rank improved from ~2.19M to ~1.3M — tiny in absolute terms but the
fastest-growing footprint of the five this week.

## Not a pricing change, but worth flagging: AI-disclosure legal exposure
Pie (well-funded, ex-Square/Toast founders, $23.7M raised) launched a
competing product also called **"Front Desk"** — an AI voice receptionist.
Separately, *Taylor v. ConverseNow* — a suit over a caller not being told
they were talking to AI — survived motion to dismiss, and CA/TX now require
call-time AI disclosure. Neither is new-this-week news, but neither was in
last week's report and both are directly relevant to how Dawnpipe's own
voice agent opens a call.

## Recommendations (proposals only)
1. **Verify Dawnpipe's own IVR script discloses "AI" per CA/TX disclosure
   law.** The ConverseNow suit is the first real litigation signal in this
   space — cheap to confirm, expensive to ignore if wrong.
2. **A "no add-on tax" angle could land now.** Dialzara just started
   charging separately for SMS/chat/outbound; Dawnpipe's all-included
   lead-gen bundle is a sharper contrast than it was last week.
3. **Watch, don't act:** if Smith.ai's no-overage annual tier is real, it's a
   direct hedge against the "surprise overage" pitch AI-native competitors
   use against incumbents. Re-verify next week before reacting.
