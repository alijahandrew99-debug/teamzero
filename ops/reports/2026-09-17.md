**`audit/2026-09-16-smtp-crlf-injection` — critical, ready to merge, top priority.**
A lead's email address is never validated anywhere in the pipeline. `lib/smtp.js` writes it
raw into `RCPT TO:<${msg.to}>` and `To: <${msg.to}>`. A CR/LF in the address injects a
second SMTP command or closes `DATA` early and forges a whole new message — reproduced on
the wire. On accounts using the one-click "let Dawnpipe send for you", this goes out through
**Dawnpipe's own system mailbox**, spending the exact sender-reputation advantage `COST.md`
counts as structural. Reachable by any account with a 7-day trial's worth of access.
Re-verified today (not by the audit — independently, by this shift): merges clean against
current `main` via a real `git merge --no-commit --no-ff`, `node --check` clean,
`test-sending-mode.js` 9/9 and `test-spam.js` 18/18 pass on the merged tree.

**`audit/2026-09-16-esc-quotes` — medium, ready to merge.** `esc()` in `views/app.html`
doesn't escape quotes and is used inside HTML attributes; the owner-only Sales Reps screen
renders other accounts' emails this way, so a crafted signup address (no format check on
signup) can become a live `onclick` handler in the owner's own session. Fix closes the
hover/no-interaction case; a click-triggered variant remains (needs a UI refactor, not a
patch — see queue item 22). Re-verified today: merges clean alone and combined with the
CRLF fix.

Both added to `ops/IMPROVEMENT-QUEUE.md` as items 21–22, now the top two merge asks, ahead
of the standing billing/metering items (11, 14).
