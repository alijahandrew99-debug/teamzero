## Also verified this shift
- **Queue item 2** (email verification on signup): confirmed still fully
  open — no verification concept anywhere in `lib/auth.js`, and
  `POST /signup` activates the account immediately. This is customer-
  facing (changes every new signup's experience), so per the keeper's
  standing rule it's proposed here, not implemented:
  - Add a `verifiedAt: null` field to new accounts; send a verification
    link via the account's own outbound path at signup; require
    verification before [something] — the "before what" is the real
    product decision:
    - **Block nothing, badge it**: account works immediately, unverified
      accounts get a dashboard banner + a resend link. Lowest funnel
      risk, weakest anti-farming effect.
    - **Block sending, not the trial**: full app access, but the AI
      can't send its first message/place its first outbound call until
      verified. Stops the specific abuse (burning paid lead/call quota
      on farmed trials) without blocking anyone from exploring the
      product.
    - **Block everything until verified**: strongest anti-fraud, highest
      funnel-conversion risk on a $399/mo cold-start product.
  My read: the middle option (block sending, not signup) matches what
  AUDIT.md's stated goal actually needs — stopping quota-burning
  farming — without touching activation-funnel conversion. But this is
  your call, not mine to make silently.
