## Shipped

**`keeper/2026-09-04-unique-tmp-write`** — `lib/db.js`'s `write()` used a
fixed `f + '.tmp'` temp filename. Two writers sharing `DATA_DIR` (an
overlapping Render deploy, or ever running >1 instance against the same
disk) would race on that same path; the loser's `renameSync` can splice
its own write over the winner's, and `read()`'s swallowed JSON-parse
failure turns that into a silently emptied collection on the next read.
Surfaced by the independent code-audit process's 2026-09-02 run as
something it flagged but couldn't confirm the trigger for from inside the
repo. Made the temp name unique per call instead of waiting on that
answer — no behavior change for a single writer, removes the collision
either way. `node --check` clean, `test-reps.js` 37/37, plus a manual
round-trip/no-leftover-tmp-files smoke test.
