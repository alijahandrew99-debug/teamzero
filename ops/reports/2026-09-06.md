## Shipped

**`keeper/2026-09-06-transfer-leg-cost`** — queue item 12, 4th bullet.
`estimateCost()` (`lib/voice.js`) never accounted for a transferred call's
`<Dial>` leg — Twilio bills that leg (us dialling out to the human)
separately from the caller's own leg, always at the outbound rate, so every
transferred call's shown cost understated the real bill by that leg's full
minutes (~$0.04 on a typical 5-minute transfer). `/voice/dialback` now
stamps `DialCallDuration` onto the call row when a transfer connects;
`/voice/status`'s billing block reads it back and passes it into
`estimateCost()` as a new `transferSec` parameter. `transferSec` defaults
to 0, a no-op, so every non-transferred call's cost is byte-for-byte
unchanged. `node --check` clean on both touched files; `test-reps.js`
37/37 (unaffected — doesn't exercise voice billing); a direct smoke test of
`estimateCost()` with and without a 2-minute transfer leg confirms the
delta is exactly `2 * RATE_VOICE_MIN_OUT` and that `transferSec: 0`
reproduces the prior output exactly.
