About Dawnpipe

Dawnpipe is an AI receptionist and sales team for small service businesses in the United States — plumbers, salons, roofers, auto shops, studios, and anyone else whose phone rings while their hands are full. It answers the calls you miss, books jobs straight into your calendar, texts back missed callers, finds new customers with verified email addresses, and writes the follow-ups — all under your review.

Who runs it

Dawnpipe is built and run by Alijah Skinner, based in Illinois, USA. It is a small, independent company — when you email support, a person reads it and answers.

Straight answers on billing

  • 7-day free trial on every plan. A card is required so we know you run a real business, but nothing is charged until day 8.
  • Cancel in one click, inside the app. The cancel button is never hidden behind a phone call, a chat queue, or a retention script.
  • No overage charges, ever. When a plan allowance runs out, the extra work pauses — it is never silently billed.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.

How we treat the people your AI talks to

  • Every call opens by saying it is an AI receptionist, on a recorded line where recording is on.
  • Automated cold calling of strangers is not a feature and will not become one — an AI voice is an "artificial voice" under the TCPA, so Dawnpipe only calls people who asked to be called back, with their consent stored.
  • Every outreach email includes the business's mailing address and a working unsubscribe link, and opt-outs are honored permanently across email and phone.
  • Spam callers get filtered out — and filtered calls never count against a customer's minutes.
