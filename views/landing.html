Questions an owner actually asks.

Will it say something dumb to my customers?

It can only state facts you typed in — your prices, your hours, your services. If it does not know, it says so and takes a message. It cannot invent a price, a promise, or a discount. That is enforced in the software, not in a training document.

Does it pretend to be a person?

No. It introduces itself as an AI assistant, and if someone asks, it says so plainly. Callers stay because it is useful, not because it fooled them.

What happens when I run out of minutes or leads?

It stops. No overage charges, ever. Minutes and leads reset every month.

Where do the leads come from? Is this a bought list?

No list. It researches the web live for companies matching the customers you want, finds the decision-maker, and verifies the email against a live database. If the address cannot be verified, the lead is thrown away instead of saved.

Will it spam people on my behalf?

Nothing sends without your approval. Every email carries your real business address and a working unsubscribe link, follow-ups stop the moment someone replies, and anyone who opts out is never contacted again — by email or phone.

What do I need to set it up?

Your website address and about two minutes. Paste your site, the AI reads it and fills in your business profile, you review it, done.

Is it a contract?

Month to month. Cancel in one click from your billing page. And your first month is guaranteed — if it does not do what this page says, email us and we refund it.
