Questions partners ask

How and when do I get paid?

By direct transfer — Zelle, PayPal, whatever we agree — paid monthly on the commission that's been collected. You tell us your payout handle; we never need your bank login. You're a 1099 contractor, so you handle your own taxes (and we'll issue a 1099 if you earn $600+ in a year), which is why we need a W-9 before the first payout.

Is it really recurring?

Yes. You earn 30% of each customer's payment every month for their first 24 months, then 10% for as long as they keep paying. One good month of referrals keeps paying you long after.

What does my audience get?

A discount off Dawnpipe when they use your code or link — a real reason to sign up through you instead of cold. The exact amount is set when you're onboarded.

Do I have to use Dawnpipe myself?

No, but the best partners do — it makes your content honest and your pitch obvious. You can get your own account at a partner rate.

How is my sale tracked?

Your link sets a 90-day cookie, and your code is recorded on checkout. First touch wins and it's permanent — once someone is tagged to you, no other partner can take that customer.
